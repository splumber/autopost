Terms of Service

Last updated: 2026-09-17

"Autopost Content Studio" is a self-hosted, single-account content automation tool. It is operated by its owner solely to generate, schedule, and publish original short-form video content to one TikTok account that the owner personally controls, using TikTok's official Content Posting API.

What this app does

  • Generates original narration scripts and video content using AI.
  • Publishes finished videos to the owner's own linked TikTok account, on a schedule the owner configures.
  • Reads basic account and video statistics (follower count, view counts) for the owner's own linked account only.

What this app does not do

  • It does not create or manage TikTok accounts on behalf of anyone.
  • It does not act on any TikTok account other than the one account its owner explicitly authorizes via TikTok's OAuth login flow.
  • It does not share data with any third party.
